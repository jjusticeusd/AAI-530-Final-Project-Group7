## NCAA-026 - onside

**Question:** An untouched free kick from A-35 rolls to A-46 inbounds. the kicking team recovers and tries to advance. Can the kicking team keep it and advance?

**Expected answer:** The kicking team may recover after the ball travels 10 yards; it becomes dead on the kicking team’s recovery, so the kicking team cannot advance.

**Expected behavior:** answer

**Enforcement:** A-46
